# Last successful dictation: confirmed-hint receipt

## Delivered behavior

The learning page now distinguishes future hints from the last successful
dictation's actual sent snapshot. It shows each personal confirmed term and
whether its spelling appears in returned text, with start/completion times.
Only one terms-only receipt is retained per token label: no transcript or audio
copy, no historical backfill, no private production fixture. Undo preserves the
past receipt and removes the term from future request snapshots. An in-flight
request still records what it sent before undo.

This deliberately covers the OpenAI-compatible dictation route only. Batch/live,
glossary/client hints and chat-only credentials are excluded. Literal NFC substring
matches are observations, not word-boundary matches or evidence of recognition
improvement. Failure to save a receipt never discards successful dictation; its
response header reports `unavailable` and the previous receipt remains dated.
The runbook is the API/retention contract; this file records release evidence.

The public demo adds **Try synthetic dictation receipt**, comparing against fixed
invented text without audio or a model call. Confirm the example, try the receipt,
then undo: next-request hints disappear but the past supplied snapshot remains.
